Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

PhysioManager is a private practice-management workspace operated by the physiotherapist who owns this app. This policy explains the information the app handles and how it is used.

Information in the workspace

The doctor may enter patient names, phone numbers, referral sources, dates of birth, presenting and underlying conditions, medication, medical history, goals, and treatment session dates and notes. This information is used only to maintain the doctor's patient and session records. Patients do not sign in to this app.

The doctor may also record a session amount in US dollars. The app uses these amounts and session dates to calculate the doctor's dashboard totals. Amounts are stored with the session records in Supabase and are not included in the clinical summary sent to the assessment assistant.

The patient form records age in whole years, symptoms, clinical assessment findings, and a pain-management plan. Historical dates of birth previously entered remain in the database but are not shown or sent by the current app. The doctor may include age and the clinical fields in the reviewed assessment-assistant summary before sending it to Google.

Google Calendar information

If the doctor connects Google Calendar, PhysioManager requests permission to list calendars and read calendar events. It displays calendar names and event details such as titles, dates, times, locations, and descriptions so the doctor can view and link appointments in the workspace. The app does not create, edit, or delete Google Calendar events. Event details are requested from Google when needed and are not stored as event copies in the app database. The app stores calendar and event identifiers when the doctor links an appointment to a patient.

The Google connection uses an encrypted refresh token so the doctor does not need to authorize every visit. Disconnecting Google Calendar removes the app's saved token and asks Google to revoke it.

Storage and service providers

Patient and session records, the doctor's account access, appointment links, and the encrypted Google connection are stored using Supabase. The app server is hosted by Render. These providers process information to operate the service. Data is sent over HTTPS. The workspace is restricted to its designated doctor account.

Use and sharing

PhysioManager does not sell personal information, use Google Calendar data for advertising, or use Google data to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Information is not shared except with the service providers described above, when required by law, or as necessary to protect the service.

Retention and choices

Patient and session records remain in the workspace until the doctor removes them or closes the service. Archiving a patient hides the record from the active list but does not delete it. The doctor can disconnect Google Calendar at any time from workspace settings; doing so removes the stored connection. Google account permissions can also be reviewed or revoked in the Google Account security settings.

Optional assessment assistant

When the doctor chooses to use the assessment assistant, they can review and edit a summary of selected clinical fields, the current session draft, and up to three previous sessions. The doctor must remove identifying information and confirm before sending. The reviewed clinical summary and search topic are sent to Google's Gemini API; the search topic is also sent to NCBI PubMed to retrieve literature. Free-tier Gemini content may be used by Google to improve its products. The app does not automatically send patient names, contact details, dates of birth, referral sources, or calendar event details.

Generated suggestions remain temporary unless the doctor explicitly adds an idea or the full guide to a session plan and saves it. Suggestions are drafts for clinician review and do not establish a diagnosis or record an assessment as completed. Google's API data terms apply to the submitted content.
